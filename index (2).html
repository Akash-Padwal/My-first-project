Cyber Help Centre

Choose the problem that matches yours. Each topic gives first steps you can take now.

Urgent? If someone is in immediate danger, call 112. For financial fraud in India, call 1930 and report at cybercrime.gov.in as early as possible.
I think my account has been hacked
  1. Change the password from a device you trust.
  2. Sign out of all other sessions in the account's security settings.
  3. Turn on multi-factor authentication.
  4. Remove forwarding rules and recovery details you do not recognise.
I have been scammed or lost money
  1. Call your bank now and ask them to block or freeze the transaction.
  2. Report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 as soon as possible.
  3. Keep screenshots, transaction IDs and chat logs.
  4. Do not pay anyone to "recover" the money.
I am getting suspicious messages or calls
  1. Do not click links, download attachments or call back the number.
  2. Check with the organisation through its official app or website.
  3. Save a copy as evidence, then block and report the sender.
I am being harassed or stalked online
  1. Save evidence: screenshots with dates, links and usernames.
  2. Use the platform's report and block tools.
  3. Avoid confronting the person, which can escalate things.
  4. Report at cybercrime.gov.in or 1930. In danger? Call 112.
My device has malware or I am being extorted
  1. Disconnect the device from the internet and Wi-Fi.
  2. Do not pay the ransom.
  3. Scan from another device and consult a professional.
  4. Report at cybercrime.gov.in or 1930.
My data may have leaked or I lost a device
  1. Remotely lock or erase the device using its official tracking service.
  2. Change passwords for accounts used on it.
  3. Watch your accounts and bank activity for anything unusual.

Never share these with anyone

OTP, UPI PIN, ATM or card PIN, CVV or passwords. Genuine banks, government agencies and service providers never ask for them.

    Cyber safety guides

    Strong passwords

    Use a long passphrase or a password manager, and never reuse a password across sites.

    Multi-factor authentication

    Add a second step to email, banking and social accounts. App-based codes are stronger than SMS.

    Spotting phishing

    Be wary of urgency, threats and links that do not match the sender. Visit the official site yourself.

    Updates and backups

    Turn on automatic updates. Keep three copies of important data on two media types, one offline or off-site.

    Privacy settings

    Review app permissions, limit public sharing and turn off location sharing you do not need.

    Public Wi-Fi and payments

    Avoid banking on open networks. Verify payment requests and QR codes through a separate, known channel.
